Why Sports Camp Infra Squash Courts

A high-speed squash court engineered for true wall rebound & player joint protection

Squash is an intense, high-velocity racket sport requiring perfectly flat wall rebound, non-slip sprung wooden flooring, and crystal-clear spectator visibility. Sports Camp Infra delivers complete turnkey WSF-approved squash courts — from damp-proof civil walls and hard plaster walling systems to air-cushioned maple wooden floors, 12mm glass back walls, acoustic tin redlines, and high-lux LED lights.

  • Official WSF dimensions (32ft x 21ft / 9.75m x 6.40m) with 18.5ft front wall height
  • High-density hard plaster walling for true ball rebound & zero cracking
  • Turnkey Setup: Civil work, sprung maple floor, 12mm glass back wall & LED lights
