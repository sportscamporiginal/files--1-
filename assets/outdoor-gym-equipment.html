Why Sports Camp Infra Outdoor Gyms

All-weather open-air fitness stations engineered for maximum durability & zero maintenance

Public fitness parks require equipment that withstands heavy daily usage, harsh monsoon rains, intense UV exposure, and potential vandalism without rusting or mechanical breakdown. Sports Camp Infra provides complete turnkey Outdoor Fitness Park setups — from civil foundation pad creation to heavy-duty GI steel manufacturing, self-lubricating sealed bearings, safety perimeter rubber surfacing, and instruction signage boards.

  • ISO 9001 & EN1176 compliant B-Class Galvanized Iron (GI) steel construction
  • Dual-coat UV-resistant thermosetting powder finish with anti-rust zinc phosphate priming
  • Turnkey Setup: Civil RCC foundation pads, equipment installation, safety rubber flooring & signage
