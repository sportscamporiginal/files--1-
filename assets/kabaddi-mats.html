Why Sports Camp Infra Kabaddi Mats

A Pro Kabaddi certified mat system engineered for high-impact tackles & non-slip raider acceleration

Kabaddi demands explosive footwork speed, maximum friction grip during turns, and high-density shock absorption to prevent mat displacement or joint injury during high-impact tackles and dives. Sports Camp Infra delivers complete turnkey Kabaddi court installations — from sub-floor concrete levelling to PKL-approved interlocking EVA mats (25mm to 35mm), baulk line & bonus line thermal painting, and surround protective boundary borders.

  • PKL & AKFI certified high-density (110kg/m³-120kg/m³) EVA rubber mats
  • Anti-skid waffle-embossed surface for maximum raider friction & zero mat shifting
  • Turnkey Setup: Concrete base, interlocking mat layout, line markings & arena seating
