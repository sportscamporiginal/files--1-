Why Sports Camp Infra EPDM Flooring

A seamless rubber safety floor engineered to eliminate fall injuries

Playgrounds, schools, and outdoor fitness parks demand a cushioned, non-abrasive floor that absorbs fall impacts while enduring heavy rain and Indian heat. Sports Camp Infra applies seamless wetpour EPDM rubber flooring — using high-grade polyurethane binders, 100% virgin colored rubber granules, and shock-absorbing SBR bases tailored to critical fall height requirements.

  • 100% Virgin UV-Stabilized EPDM granules in 24+ vibrant color choices
  • Custom thickness (15mm to 50mm+) matching Critical Fall Height (CFH) standards
  • Turnkey Setup: Concrete prep, polyurethane primer, SBR base & custom graphics
