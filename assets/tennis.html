COURT STRUCTURE

What's Inside Your Court?

Every tennis court is engineered layer-by-layer over precision concrete foundations to deliver impact relief and true ball performance.

01

Acrylic Topcoat & ITF Line Paint

Non-slip UV resistant acrylic color coats with polyurethane ITF boundary markings.

02

Rubberized Cushion Resurfacer

SBR rubber granules blended with acrylic binder to absorb shock and protect player joints.

03

Acrylic Resurfacer

High-grade acrylic filler that seals concrete pores and ensures a uniform surface texture.

04

Reinforced Concrete Base

Slope-graded RCC/PCC concrete slab built to withstand heavy weather and structural loads.

05

Vapor Barrier & Drainage Layer

Waterproof membrane preventing sub-soil moisture penetration and ensuring long durability.

06

Compacted Foundation

Mechanically compacted sub-grade gravel base offering dense structural support.
