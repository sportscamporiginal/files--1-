How Much Does a Basketball Court Cost in India?

Basketball court cost in India depends on the court type, location, base condition, indoor or outdoor construction, surface system, lighting and additional infrastructure. A reliable quotation should define the complete scope instead of giving only a surface rate.

Sports Camp InfraUpdated 2026-09-22Guide

Outdoor acrylic vs indoor wooden

These are fundamentally different projects. Outdoor acrylic construction focuses on weather resistance and drainage, while indoor wooden courts involve a building and specialized sub-floor system.

Base and civil work

New construction may require excavation, foundation, concrete and leveling. Existing slabs may need repair or preparation.

Surface and line marking

The selected coating or flooring system affects the budget. Court colors, markings and finishing should be included in the specification.

Lighting and accessories

Backboards, hoops, poles, lighting, fencing and spectator facilities may be separate line items.

Location and access

Transport, site access, existing structures and local labor conditions can affect project cost.

How to compare quotations

Compare material specifications, civil scope, installation, warranty, maintenance guidance and exclusions—not just the headline price.
