Pickleball Court Construction in Raipur: Complete Guide

Pickleball facilities are compact compared with many field sports, but court construction still depends on accurate layout, a stable base, suitable surfacing and good drainage. A well-planned court can be built for clubs, academies, residential communities, schools or commercial sports facilities.

Sports Camp InfraUpdated 2026-09-22Guide

Site planning

Check the available area, access, existing ground condition, surrounding structures and intended number of courts. Allow enough circulation space outside the playing lines for safe movement.

Base preparation

The court needs a stable and sufficiently level foundation. Existing concrete may need repairs or preparation before the sports coating is applied.

Acrylic surfacing

Outdoor pickleball courts commonly use layered sports coatings designed for grip, color and durability. The exact system should be selected for the site and manufacturer requirements.

Drainage

Outdoor courts should have a controlled slope and drainage route that prevents prolonged standing water. Drainage should be resolved before final surfacing.

Fencing and lighting

Perimeter fencing helps retain balls and separates the playing area from surrounding spaces. Lighting can extend operating hours, but pole locations and glare should be considered during layout.

Maintenance

Keep the court clean, inspect cracks and surface wear, maintain drainage paths and follow the coating manufacturer's cleaning recommendations.
