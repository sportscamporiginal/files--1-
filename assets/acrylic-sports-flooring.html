Why Acrylic Sports Flooring

Multi-layer synthetic courts engineered for performance & joint safety

Acrylic synthetic flooring is the global standard for outdoor and indoor sports courts. Combining 100% pure acrylic resins with rubber micro-particles, our systems deliver consistent ball bounce, high slip-resistance, and cushion layers that absorb player impact on knees and ankles.

  • 5, 7 & 8-layer cushion systems with SBR/EPDM rubber granules
  • 100% UV-stabilized pigments — fade-resistant in extreme weather
  • Multi-sport line marking: Tennis, Basketball, Pickleball & Volleyball
