How to Build a Multi-Sport Facility in Raipur

A multi-sport facility can combine several activities on one site, but successful planning requires more than placing different courts next to each other. Each sport has different dimensions, surface requirements, lighting needs and safety zones.

Sports Camp InfraUpdated 2026-09-22Guide

Start with the site master plan

Map the available land, access, parking, utilities, drainage and existing structures. Then allocate areas for each sport and leave sufficient circulation between facilities.

Group compatible surfaces

Hard courts can often be planned together, while turf-based areas need their own base and drainage strategy. Grouping facilities can simplify construction and maintenance.

Plan drainage early

Rainwater movement should be considered across the entire site. Water leaving one area should not create problems for another court or turf.

Lighting and electrical planning

A multi-sport facility can have several lighting zones. Plan pole positions, cable routes, control systems and maintenance access before construction begins.

Operations and maintenance

Storage, cleaning access, equipment rooms, staff movement and waste collection are easy to overlook. Designing them into the site improves long-term operations.

Future expansion

If more courts or facilities may be added later, reserve service corridors and utility capacity where practical.
