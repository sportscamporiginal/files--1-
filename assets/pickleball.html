COURT STRUCTURE

What's Inside Your Court?

Every pickleball court is constructed layer-by-layer over precision concrete bases to ensure durability and true ball bounce.

01

Acrylic Playing Surface

Premium non-slip acrylic surface providing excellent grip, smooth gameplay and sharp USAPA lines.

02

Rubber Cushion Layer

Flexible SBR rubber granules engineered to absorb impact, reduce player fatigue, and protect joints.

03

Acrylic Resurfacer

Acrylic binder filler layer that seals concrete pores and ensures a smooth, crack-free finish.

04

Reinforced Concrete Base

Precision-finished RCC/PCC concrete base slab providing strength and efficient water runoff.

05

Compacted Foundation

Mechanically compacted sub-grade crushed stone aggregate prepared for long-term stability.
