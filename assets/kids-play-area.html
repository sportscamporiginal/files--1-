PLAY AREA STRUCTURE

What's Inside Your Play Area?

Every kids play area is constructed layer-by-layer over precision concrete base slabs to ensure shock absorption, comfort and safety.

01

Virgin EPDM Top Layer

10mm virgin EPDM colored rubber granules providing vibrant aesthetics, non-slip friction, and weather resistance.

02

SBR Shock Cushion Base

15mm–40mm high-density recycled SBR rubber shock layer absorbing foot landing and accidental fall impacts.

03

Polyurethane Binder

Waterproof polyurethane resin bonding rubber granules seamlessly into an all-weather elastic floor.

04

Waterproof Membrane

Protective sub-base barrier preventing ground dampness and increasing flooring lifespan.

05

Concrete Base (Sloped)

Precision-finished reinforced concrete base with proper slope for structural strength and water drainage.

06

Compacted Aggregate Base

Mechanically compacted crushed stone aggregate layer providing a stable foundation.

07

Compacted Natural Soil

Properly compacted soil prepared to support heavy GI steel anchor footings and play equipment.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Find answers to common questions about kids play area equipment, EPDM rubber safety flooring, plastic safety and our installation services.

Our play systems use imported food-grade, non-toxic UV-stabilized LLDPE virgin plastic for slides and roofs, 114mm heavy galvanized steel pipes with powder coating for main support posts, and 304 Grade Stainless Steel anti-rust bolts.

EPDM rubber flooring provides critical shock absorption that prevents head, knee, and joint injuries if a child stumbles or falls from slides or swings. It is non-slip, all-weather, fast-draining, and available in vibrant custom colors.

For play equipment with deck heights up to 4 feet, a 25mm to 35mm EPDM rubber floor is recommended. For taller multi-play castle towers (6 feet to 10 feet deck heights), a 45mm to 50mm thick dual-layer EPDM floor is installed to meet international Critical Fall Height (CFH) safety ratings.

Yes! All plastic components feature UV-8 stabilizers to prevent sun fading, brittleness, and heat retention. All steel pipes are zinc-galvanized inside out to prevent internal rusting during heavy monsoons.

Civil foundation grouting and EPDM rubber flooring installation take 3 to 5 days, followed by 2 to 3 days of equipment assembly and safety inspection.
