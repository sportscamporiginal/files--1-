TURF STRUCTURE

What's Inside Your Football Turf?

Every football pitch is built layer-by-layer over precision gravel drainage foundations to ensure player safety and all-weather playability.

01

White FIFA Line Tufting

Integrated white synthetic grass lines woven directly into the carpet for permanent boundary markings.

02

50mm–60mm Monofilament Turf

UV-stabilized anti-abrasive synthetic grass fibers engineered for resilience and natural ball roll.

03

SBR Rubber Granule Infill

Cryogenic SBR rubber granules providing springiness, player shock absorption, and slide protection.

04

Rounded Silica Sand Infill

Clean washed silica sand layer ballast keeping grass blades upright and stabilizing the turf backing.

05

Shock Pad & Geotextile Fabric

Porous geotextile membrane preventing sub-soil mixing while allowing rapid rainwater drainage.

06

Crushed Aggregate Sub-Base

Two-tier graded aggregate gravel base with perforated drain pipes for zero-waterlogging performance.
