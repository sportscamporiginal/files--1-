TECHNICAL ANATOMY

What's Inside Our Sports Courts?

Every SportsCamp Infra facility is constructed layer-by-layer over precision sloped concrete slabs to guarantee ball bounce, cushioning, and durability.

01

Top Wear Surface

Non-glare ITF/BWF/USAPA certified acrylic coat, BWF PVC mat, or monofilament synthetic turf layer.

02

Silica & Rubber Cushion Layer

Multi-layer rubber granule undertones or round silica sand infill providing resilient foot impact absorption.

03

Resin Primer & Bonding Agent

High-penetration primer ensuring permanent adhesion between sub-base concrete and surface layers.

04

Waterproof Damp-Proof Membrane

Protective sub-base barrier preventing ground dampness and rising moisture damage.

05

Sloped Reinforced Concrete Base

Precision laser-finished M25 grade reinforced concrete slab with 0.5% slope for rapid rainwater runoff.

06

Compacted Aggregate Sub-Base

Mechanically compacted 150mm crushed stone aggregate layer offering high structural load capacity.

07

Compacted Natural Soil

Properly compacted sub-soil foundation prepared to support heavy steel posts and play structures.
