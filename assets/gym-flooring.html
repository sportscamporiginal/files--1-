Why Sports Camp Infra Gym Flooring

Flooring engineered for sub-floor protection & structural sound isolation

Commercial weightlifting zones and Crossfit boxes require flooring that withstands continuous 200kg+ barbell drops without cracking concrete sub-floors or transmitting structural noise to neighboring spaces. Sports Camp Infra provides complete turnkey gym flooring — from moisture barriers and acoustic rubber underlayment to high-density SBR rubber tiles, Crossfit turf tracks, transition ramps, and custom brand logos.

  • High-density SBR rubber tiles & rolls (10mm to 50mm thickness) with EPDM speckles
  • Heavy weight drop pads engineered for max shock reduction & zero sub-floor damage
  • Turnkey Setup: Base prep, polyurethane bonding, Crossfit sprint turf & bevel ramps
