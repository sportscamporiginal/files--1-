TECHNICAL ANATOMY

What's Inside Your Running Track?

Every SportsCamp Infra running track is built layer-by-layer over precision asphalt or concrete bases to ensure force reduction, water drainage, and longevity.

01

Virgin EPDM Top Granule Layer (3mm–6mm)

High-purity colored virgin EPDM rubber granules (1-4mm size) providing non-slip grip, UV resistance, and spike durability.

02

Polyurethane Pore Sealer Coat

MDI polyurethane pore sealer locking the base cushion into a non-porous, waterproof unified structure.

03

SBR Shock Cushion Base Layer (7mm–10mm)

In-situ poured recycled SBR rubber granules bound with polyurethane resin absorbing foot impact forces.

04

Polyurethane Bonding Primer

High-tack primer ensuring permanent chemical adhesion between asphalt/concrete and synthetic rubber layers.

05

Sloped Asphalt / Concrete Sub-Base

Precision laser-finished asphalt (bituminous concrete) or M25 reinforced concrete slab with 0.5%–1% transverse drainage slope.

06

Compacted Crushed Stone Base

Mechanically compacted 150mm–200mm crushed aggregate base providing structural load distribution and sub-drainage.

07

Compacted Sub-Grade Soil

Stabilized and compacted natural earth sub-grade supporting the entire athletic facility structure.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Find answers to common questions about synthetic running track systems, Sandwich vs Full PUR, thickness, and IAAF standards.

The 13mm Sandwich System consists of a 7mm SBR shock cushion base layer topped with a 6mm virgin EPDM colored wear layer, making it ideal for schools and universities. The 13mm Full PUR (Full Pour Polyurethane) System is made of 100% solid liquid polyurethane resin and virgin EPDM granules throughout all layers with zero SBR fillers, offering maximum spike resistance and WA Class-1 competition ratings.

As per World Athletics (IAAF) guidelines, the standard total thickness for athletic running tracks is 13mm (7mm base cushion + 6mm top wear layer for Sandwich System, or 13mm solid PU/EPDM matrix for Full PUR System).

Synthetic running tracks require a smooth, well-compacted sub-base consisting of a 150mm crushed aggregate layer topped with a 40mm–60mm bituminous concrete (asphalt) slab or M25 reinforced concrete slab with a 0.5%–1% transverse drainage slope.

Yes! Both our Sandwich and Full PUR track systems are engineered to withstand standard 6mm to 9mm athletic track spikes without tearing or surface degradation.

Civil sub-base asphalt preparation takes 15 to 25 days, followed by 10 to 15 days of synthetic rubber surfacing, curing, and IAAF certified line marking.
