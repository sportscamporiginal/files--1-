FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Find answers to common questions about kids play area equipment, EPDM rubber safety flooring, plastic safety, and warranty terms.

Our play systems use imported food-grade, non-toxic UV-stabilized LLDPE virgin plastic for slides and roofs, 114mm heavy galvanized steel pipes with dual-layer powder coating for main support posts, and 304 Grade Stainless Steel anti-rust bolts.

EPDM rubber flooring provides critical shock absorption that prevents head, knee, and joint injuries if a child stumbles or falls from slides or swings. It is non-slip, all-weather, fast-draining, and available in vibrant custom colors.

For play equipment with deck heights up to 4 feet, a 25mm to 35mm EPDM rubber floor is recommended. For taller multi-play castle towers (6 feet to 10 feet deck heights), a 45mm to 50mm thick dual-layer EPDM floor is installed to meet international Critical Fall Height (CFH) safety ratings.

Yes! All plastic components feature UV-8 stabilizers to prevent sun fading, brittleness, and heat retention. All steel pipes are zinc-galvanized inside out to prevent internal rusting during heavy monsoons.

Civil foundation grouting and EPDM rubber flooring installation take 3 to 5 days, followed by 2 to 3 days of equipment assembly and safety inspection.
