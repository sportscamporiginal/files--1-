Why Sports Camp Infra Pickleball Courts

Courts engineered for true ball pop, quick pivots & zero knee fatigue

Pickleball is India’s fastest-growing racket sport. It demands consistent polymer ball bounce, crisp non-volley zone (“Kitchen”) markings, and non-slip surface texture during fast dink exchanges. Sports Camp Infra delivers end-to-end court construction — from slope-controlled RCC sub-base to 5-8 coat liquid rubber acrylic surfacing, heavy-duty net post sleeves, perimeter fencing, and LED lights.

  • USAPA-standard 44ft x 20ft court footprint with 7ft Kitchen zone markings
  • Multi-layer 100% acrylic resurfacer & SBR liquid rubber cushion for joint protection
  • Turnkey Arenas: Civil base, heavy-duty net posts, chainlink fencing & LED lighting
