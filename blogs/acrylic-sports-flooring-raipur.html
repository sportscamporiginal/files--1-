Acrylic Sports Flooring in Raipur: Benefits, Types & Applications

Acrylic sports flooring is widely used for outdoor and some indoor sports courts because it can be built as a multi-layer surface over a properly prepared base. The final result depends heavily on the condition of the foundation, coating system and installation process.

Sports Camp InfraUpdated 2026-09-22Guide

Where acrylic flooring is used

Acrylic systems are commonly considered for basketball, tennis, multi-purpose and other hard courts. The coating can be customized in colors and court markings according to the facility.

Layer-by-layer construction

A typical system may include primer, resurfacer, cushion or filler layers, color coats and line marking. The exact layers depend on the product system and performance requirements.

Base preparation

Cracks, uneven areas and moisture problems should be addressed before surfacing. A clean and stable base is essential for adhesion and long-term performance.

Outdoor drainage

The court's slope and surrounding drainage should be designed so rainwater leaves the surface efficiently. Coating alone cannot fix poor site drainage.

Color and branding

Court colors can be selected to improve visual contrast and coordinate with a school's, club's or academy's identity. Line colors should remain easy to see.

Maintenance

Regular cleaning, inspection of cracks and prompt repairs help maintain the finish. Heavy equipment and unsuitable cleaning chemicals should be avoided unless approved by the coating manufacturer.
