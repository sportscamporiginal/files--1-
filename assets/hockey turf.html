FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Find answers to the most common questions about synthetic hockey pitch construction, FIH standards, and grass options.

The cost depends on pitch type (Water-Dressed vs Sand-Dressed), turf choice (Bellin Hockey Supreme vs Bellin Supreme 18mm), shock pad layer thickness, sub-base civil prep, water cannon system, and LED sports lighting. Contact us for a free site inspection and detailed quote.

Water-Dressed pitches (Bellin Hockey Supreme) use dense unfilled turf wetted with water prior to play, offering maximum ball speed and zero skin friction for international stadiums. Sand-Dressed pitches (Bellin Supreme 18mm) use silica sand infill, offering high durability and cost-effective operation for schools and academies.

Official FIH pitch dimensions are 91.40 metres (100 yards) in length by 55.00 metres (60 yards) in width, with a shooting circle radius of 14.63 metres (16 yards).

A 15mm in-situ rubber shock pad underlayment absorbs player landing impact energy, protecting knees and ankles from stress injuries while maintaining uniform ball bounce across the pitch.

A full-size FIH hockey pitch construction typically takes between 30 to 45 days including earth excavation, porous asphalt sub-base, shock pad laying, turf fitting, line tufting, and water sprinkler installation.
