EPDM Flooring for Sports & Play Areas: Complete Guide

EPDM flooring is commonly considered for areas where impact attenuation, resilience and outdoor durability are important. It can be used in selected play and recreation environments, but the right build-up depends on the intended use and safety requirements.

Sports Camp InfraUpdated 2026-09-22Guide

What EPDM flooring is

EPDM is a synthetic rubber material used in resilient surfacing systems. It can be installed in different thicknesses and configurations depending on the application.

Where it can be used

Applications may include play areas, gym environments, walking or recreation zones and other spaces where a resilient surface is desired. The exact suitability should be assessed for the activity.

Base preparation

The base needs to be stable, clean and properly prepared. Drainage and water movement should be considered before installation.

Safety considerations

For children's play areas, impact performance and applicable safety requirements should be evaluated as part of the project specification.

Color and design

EPDM granules can support multiple color combinations and visual zones. Designs can be coordinated with school or facility branding.

Maintenance

Regular cleaning, inspection for damaged sections and keeping drainage paths clear help maintain the surface.
