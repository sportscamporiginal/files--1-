Why Sports Camp Infra Table Tennis Halls

An indoor TT arena engineered for rapid footwork & glare-free ball tracking

Professional Table Tennis requires non-slip flooring that absorbs lateral footwork shock without shoe slipping or floor reflection under bright lights. Sports Camp Infra delivers complete turnkey Table Tennis Hall installations — from smooth sub-floor concrete prep to ITTF-certified red/blue embossed PVC rubber mats, acoustic walling, 500 Lux anti-glare LED light grids, and court surround barriers.

  • ITTF-certified 4.5mm to 7.0mm multi-layer PVC flooring with high-density shock foam
  • Official court footprint (14m x 7m international / 10m x 5m national standard)
  • Turnkey Setup: Sub-base, PVC flooring, surround barrier screens & 500 Lux lights
