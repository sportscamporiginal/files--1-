Why Sports Camp Infra Kids Play Flooring

A soft safety surface engineered to eliminate playground fall injuries

Children playing on swings, slides, and climbing frames need a soft, non-abrasive floor that cushions tumbles and prevents head or joint injuries. Sports Camp Infra provides complete turnkey safety flooring — using EN1177 certified non-toxic EPDM rubber wetpour, soft SBR cushion underlayment, heat-resistant play turf, and custom interactive graphics like hopscotch grids, animal figures, and math tracks.

  • 100% Non-toxic, lead-free, odorless EPDM rubber & soft play turf options
  • Critical Fall Height (CFH) certified thickness (25mm to 50mm+) for zero injury play
  • Turnkey Setup: Concrete base, shock pad underlay, 2D/3D game graphics & borders
