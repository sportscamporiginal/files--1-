Who We Are

A trusted name in sport, built over two decades

Welcome to Sports Camp Infra — Chhattisgarh's leading sports and goods store, proudly located in Raipur. Established in 2010, we've spent over two decades as a trusted destination for sports enthusiasts and fitness lovers across the state.

With a strong commitment to quality and customer satisfaction, we've served over 1 million customers and built lasting relationships with schools, colleges, institutes, clubs and individuals. Our range of 5,000+ sports and fitness products ensures that everyone — from first-time players to seasoned professionals — finds exactly what they need under one roof.

Our catalogue spans cricket, football, badminton, volleyball, athletics, gym and fitness, indoor games and much more. Whether it's outfitting a school's entire sports programme or helping an individual find the right gear, our experienced team is always on hand with expert advice and dependable service.

We also specialise in customised sports solutions — tailor-made cages and nets, outdoor gym installations, and complete sports flooring projects — because whatever the sport, we're equipped to deliver the perfect setup.
