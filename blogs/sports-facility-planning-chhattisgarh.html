How to Plan a Professional Sports Facility in Chhattisgarh

A professional sports facility begins with a clear brief: who will use it, which sports will be offered, how much land is available and how the facility will operate. Careful planning before construction can prevent expensive changes later.

Sports Camp InfraUpdated 2026-09-22Guide

Define the business or institutional goal

A school, sports academy, club and commercial turf operator can have very different requirements. Define the users, operating hours, expected traffic and revenue or program objectives.

Survey the site

Document land dimensions, levels, soil, access, utilities, drainage and existing structures. These facts shape the feasibility of the project.

Develop the master plan

Place courts, turfs, buildings, parking, circulation and service areas on one plan. Reserve space for drainage and future expansion.

Choose surfaces and specifications

Select the surface according to sport, usage, environment and maintenance capacity. Avoid choosing solely by appearance.

Budget in phases

Separate civil work, sports surfaces, fencing, lighting, buildings and optional amenities. This makes it easier to phase construction.

Plan operations and maintenance

A facility is an operating asset. Include cleaning, grooming, drainage, inspections, equipment storage and future repairs in the plan.

Construction and handover

Use a defined installation sequence and inspect each stage. Collect product information, maintenance guidance and warranty documentation at handover.
