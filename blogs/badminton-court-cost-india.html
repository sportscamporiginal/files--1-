How Much Does a Badminton Court Cost in India?

The cost of a badminton facility depends on much more than the court surface. Flooring, building structure, lighting, ceiling height, ventilation, circulation and the number of courts can all affect the project.

Sports Camp InfraUpdated 2026-09-22Guide

Single court or multi-court facility

A single court may be suitable for a small academy or private facility, while multi-court projects require more structural, electrical and circulation planning.

Flooring system

The flooring specification affects playing comfort, maintenance and installation requirements. Choose a system designed for badminton rather than a general-purpose floor.

Building requirements

For an indoor facility, roof height, lighting, ventilation and moisture control should be planned with the courts.

Electrical and lighting

Badminton requires lighting that supports visibility without creating distracting glare. Electrical planning should be integrated into the building design.

Additional facilities

Changing rooms, reception, storage, seating and washrooms can materially affect the total project scope.

Getting a project estimate

Provide the number of courts, building status, floor area, city and desired facilities. A site inspection will help identify construction requirements.
